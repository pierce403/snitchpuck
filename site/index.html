01 / RESEARCH

Research by Reynaldo and nyx

Rey found a HALO sensor on his high school’s network and later obtained a secondhand unit to examine.

His BSidesPDX 2025 presentation describes the project’s origins. [5]

DEF CON 33 / AUGUST 2025

Reynaldo and nyx disassembled the unit, extracted its firmware and analyzed its software. They presented the findings at DEF CON 33 on August 8, 2025. [1] [8]

WIRED reported that an attacker on the device’s network could guess weak administrator passwords and install custom firmware. The researchers demonstrated live listening through the sensor’s microphones. [2]

The demonstration does not establish that installed devices recorded occupants. The vendor’s August 2025 advisory said it knew of no exploitation in customer environments. [3]

02 / DEVICE

What HALO detects

HALO is marketed for vape detection, air quality monitoring and safety alerts.

Vendor-advertised audio features include selected spoken keywords, critical noises and gunshot detection. [4] Motorola’s 2024 solution brief describes HALO as not using visual or audio recording. [9]

04 / DATES

Timeline

2023 → 2025
  1. JUN 01, 2023

    Housing case study published

    A vendor case study describes 12 HALO devices installed in Saratoga Springs public-housing apartments earlier that year to enforce a nonsmoking policy. [6]

  2. DEC 18, 2023

    Motorola acquires IPVideo

    Motorola Solutions announces its acquisition of IPVideo, the company behind HALO. [7]

  3. AUG 04, 2025

    Security advisory published

    The vendor publishes affected firmware versions and identifies version 2.17.0.2 as addressing the vulnerabilities. [3]

  4. AUG 08, 2025

    DEF CON presentation

    Reynaldo and nyx present the research at DEF CON 33. WIRED publishes its report the same day. [8] [2]

05 / FIRMWARE

August 2025 firmware update

The advisory lists the following affected versions and names firmware 2.17.0.2 as the fix.

Cloud-connected units were to update automatically, except where customers opted out. Standalone units require manual installation. [3]

AFFECTED VERSIONS IN THE AUG 04, 2025 ADVISORY

HALO 3C2.15.0.13 and earlier
HALO 2 / 2C2.14.3.0 and earlier
